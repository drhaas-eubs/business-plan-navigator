A framework used without attribution is an unevidenced assertion, and the rubric treats it as one. Students should cite the originating author on first use of every model — Weihrich for TOWS, Doran for SMART, Barney for VRIO — and reserve secondary textbook citations for the teaching form of a framework rather than its origin. The gallery below gives the anchor reference for each.
⌕
FJA

Francis J. Aguilar

1932–2013
Introduced environmental scanning, the ancestor of PESTEL, arguing that firms fail from outside-in forces they never monitored.
PESTEL Analysis
Aguilar, F.J. (1967) Scanning the business environment. New York: Macmillan.
MA

Michael Alley

b. 1957
Engineering communication scholar whose assertion–evidence structure replaced the bulleted topic slide with a sentence headline and a visual.
Assertion–Evidence Structure
Alley, M. (2013) The craft of scientific presentations: critical steps to succeed and critical errors to avoid. 2nd edn. New York: Springer.
HIA

H. Igor Ansoff

1918–2002
Father of strategic management; the growth matrix remains the standard grammar for describing where growth will come from.
Ansoff Growth Matrix
Ansoff, H.I. (1957) 'Strategies for diversification', Harvard Business Review, 35(5), pp. 113–124.
JBB

Jay B. Barney

b. 1954
Formalised the resource-based view: advantage comes from resources that are valuable, rare, inimitable and organisationally supported.
VRIO Framework
Barney, J.B. (1991) 'Firm resources and sustained competitive advantage', Journal of Management, 17(1), pp. 99–120.
RMB

R. Meredith Belbin

b. 1926
Showed that team effectiveness depends on a balance of behavioural roles rather than on individual brilliance.
Belbin Team Roles
Belbin, R.M. (2010) Team roles at work. 2nd edn. Oxford: Butterworth-Heinemann.
CSF

Craig S. Fleisher & Babette E. Bensoussan

2015
Consolidated the competitive intelligence toolkit, including perceptual mapping, into a single applied reference.
Perceptual Mapping
Fleisher, C.S. and Bensoussan, B.E. (2015) Business and competitive analysis: effective application of new and classic methods. 2nd edn. Upper Saddle River, NJ: Pearson FT Press.
BHB

Bernard H. Booms & Mary Jo Bitner

1981
Extended the marketing mix to seven elements for services, adding people, process and physical evidence.
7Ps Services Marketing Mix
Booms, B.H. and Bitner, M.J. (1981) 'Marketing strategies and organization structures for service firms', in Donnelly, J.H. and George, W.R. (eds.) Marketing of services. Chicago: American Marketing Association, pp. 47–51.
SB

Steve Blank

b. 1953
Originated customer development and the discipline of sizing markets bottom-up before building anything.
TAM / SAM / SOM · Customer Development
Blank, S. and Dorf, B. (2012) The startup owner's manual: the step-by-step guide for building a great company. Pescadero, CA: K&S Ranch.
KB

Kurt Bostelaar

contemporary
Strategyzer programme designer and founder of Leverlo; his message-design approach turns complex propositions into a single clear, visual and practical message.
Message Strategy
Bostelaar, K. (2023) Making the complex simple, visual and practical: a message design approach. Leverlo. Available at: https://www.leverlo.com (Accessed: 2 August 2026).
RCC

Robert C. Camp

b. 1935
Xerox engineer who formalised benchmarking as a repeatable method for closing measured performance gaps against best-in-class comparators.
Benchmarking
Camp, R.C. (1989) Benchmarking: the search for industry best practices that lead to superior performance. Milwaukee, WI: ASQC Quality Press.
CMC

Clayton M. Christensen

1952–2020
Disruption theorist whose jobs-to-be-done lens reframed demand around customer progress rather than customer demographics.
Jobs to Be Done
Christensen, C.M., Hall, T., Dillon, K. and Duncan, D.S. (2016) Competing against luck: the story of innovation and customer choice. New York: HarperBusiness.
FRD

Fred R. David

1949–2021
Strategic management textbook author whose competitive profile matrix turned rival comparison into a weighted, auditable score.
Competitive Profile Matrix
David, F.R. and David, F.R. (2017) Strategic management: a competitive advantage approach, concepts and cases. 16th edn. Harlow: Pearson.
JD

John Doerr

b. 1951
Carried Andy Grove's objectives-and-key-results system into venture practice as a quarterly execution layer.
OKR
Doerr, J. (2018) Measure what matters: OKRs — the simple idea that drives 10x growth. London: Portfolio Penguin.
GTD

George T. Doran

1981
Coined the SMART criteria in a two-page management article that has outlived almost everything published alongside it.
SMART Objectives
Doran, G.T. (1981) 'There's a S.M.A.R.T. way to write management's goals and objectives', Management Review, 70(11), pp. 35–36.
ND

Nancy Duarte

b. 1963
Analysed hundreds of great speeches to derive the sparkline: a rhythm between what is and what could be.
Sparkline Narrative
Duarte, N. (2010) Resonate: present visual stories that transform audiences. Hoboken, NJ: John Wiley & Sons.
HLG

Henry L. Gantt

1861–1919
Scientific-management engineer whose bar schedule became the universal visual language of project time.
Gantt Chart
Gantt, H.L. (1919) Organizing for work. New York: Harcourt, Brace and Howe.
MSH

Michael S. Hunt

1972
Coined the term strategic group in his Harvard dissertation on the US home appliance industry, showing that rivalry concentrates within clusters rather than across a whole industry.
Strategic Group Mapping
Hunt, M.S. (1972) Competition in the major home appliance industry, 1960–1970. Unpublished doctoral dissertation. Harvard University.
JPW

James P. Womack & Daniel T. Jones

2003
Brought lean thinking from Toyota's shop floor into general management practice.
Lean Thinking
Womack, J.P. and Jones, D.T. (2003) Lean thinking: banish waste and create wealth in your corporation. Rev. edn. London: Simon & Schuster.
PK

Philip Kotler

b. 1931
Systematised marketing management; segmentation, targeting and positioning remain his most durable contribution.
STP
Kotler, P. and Keller, K.L. (2016) Marketing management. 15th edn. Harlow: Pearson.
ESE

Elias St. Elmo Lewis

1872–1948
Advertising pioneer credited with the attention–interest–desire–action hierarchy.
AIDA Funnel
Lewis, E.St.E. (1908) Financial advertising. Indianapolis: Levey Bros.
WCK

W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne

2005
Argued that value innovation escapes competition rather than winning it, and gave the strategy canvas as its instrument.
Blue Ocean Strategy
Kim, W.C. and Mauborgne, R. (2005) Blue ocean strategy: how to create uncontested market space and make the competition irrelevant. Boston, MA: Harvard Business School Press.
REM

Richard E. Mayer

b. 1947
Cognitive psychologist whose multimedia learning principles are the evidence base behind every rule about slide density.
Cognitive Load Principles
Mayer, R.E. (2009) Multimedia learning. 2nd edn. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.
EJM

E. Jerome McCarthy

1928–2015
Reduced the marketing decision space to four controllable variables, an act of compression still taught worldwide.
4Ps Marketing Mix
McCarthy, E.J. (1960) Basic marketing: a managerial approach. Homewood, IL: Richard D. Irwin.
DM

Dave McClure

b. 1966
Reframed the funnel for digital ventures as five measurable stages with memorable naming.
AARRR Pirate Metrics
McClure, D. (2007) Startup metrics for pirates: AARRR! 500 Startups. Available at: https://www.slideshare.net/dmc500hats (Accessed: 2 August 2026).
JM

J.D. Meier

contemporary
Author and practitioner whose thumbnail thinking forces structural clarity by shrinking each idea to a frame that can be sketched by hand.
Thumbnail Thinking
Meier, J.D. (2025) Thumbnail thinking: sketch your way to clarity. Available at: https://jdmeier.com (Accessed: 2 August 2026).
FMM

Franco Modigliani & Merton H. Miller

1958
Nobel laureates whose capital-structure propositions underpin every discount rate a business plan uses.
WACC & Capital Structure
Modigliani, F. and Miller, M.H. (1958) 'The cost of capital, corporation finance and the theory of investment', American Economic Review, 48(3), pp. 261–297.
BM

Barbara Minto

b. 1930
The first female consultant at McKinsey; her pyramid principle is the grammar of answer-first business communication.
Pyramid Principle
Minto, B. (2009) The pyramid principle: logic in writing and thinking. 3rd edn. Harlow: Financial Times Prentice Hall.
HM

Henry Mintzberg

b. 1939
Showed that structure is a configuration matched to environment and stage, not a default hierarchy.
Organisational Configurations
Mintzberg, H. (1979) The structuring of organizations. Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice Hall.
TTN

Thomas T. Nagle & Georg Müller

2018
Made value-based pricing operational by quantifying reference value and differentiation value.
Value-Based Pricing
Nagle, T.T. and Müller, G. (2018) The strategy and tactics of pricing: a guide to growing more profitably. 6th edn. New York: Routledge.
RSK

Robert S. Kaplan & David P. Norton

1996
Broke the monopoly of financial measurement by adding customer, process and learning perspectives.
Balanced Scorecard
Kaplan, R.S. and Norton, D.P. (1996) The balanced scorecard: translating strategy into action. Boston, MA: Harvard Business School Press.
AOY

Alexander Osterwalder & Yves Pigneur

2010
Turned business model design into a shared visual language used by more than five million practitioners.
Business Model Canvas · Value Proposition Canvas
Osterwalder, A. and Pigneur, Y. (2010) Business model generation: a handbook for visionaries, game changers, and challengers. Hoboken, NJ: John Wiley & Sons.
JCC

James C. Collins & Jerry I. Porras

1996
Separated enduring core ideology from the envisioned future, giving vision and mission statements analytical structure.
Vision, Mission & Core Values
Collins, J.C. and Porras, J.I. (1996) 'Building your company's vision', Harvard Business Review, 74(5), pp. 65–77.
MEP

Michael E. Porter

b. 1947
The dominant figure in competitive strategy: industry structure, generic strategies and the value chain.
Five Forces · Generic Strategies · Value Chain
Porter, M.E. (1985) Competitive advantage: creating and sustaining superior performance. New York: Free Press.
ER

Eric Ries

b. 1978
Recast the startup as an experiment-running institution, with the MVP as its central instrument.
Lean Startup / MVP
Ries, E. (2011) The lean startup: how constant innovation creates radically successful businesses. London: Portfolio Penguin.
EMR

Everett M. Rogers

1931–2004
Mapped how innovations spread through adopter categories, explaining the shape of every credible sales ramp.
Diffusion of Innovations
Rogers, E.M. (2003) Diffusion of innovations. 5th edn. New York: Free Press.
EHS

Edgar H. Schein

1928–2023
Distinguished artefacts, espoused values and basic assumptions, exposing the gap between stated and lived culture.
Levels of Culture
Schein, E.H. and Schein, P.A. (2017) Organizational culture and leadership. 5th edn. Hoboken, NJ: John Wiley & Sons.
GLS

G. Lynn Shostack

b. 1943
Invented the service blueprint, making invisible service processes designable and measurable.
Service Blueprint
Shostack, G.L. (1984) 'Designing services that deliver', Harvard Business Review, 62(1), pp. 133–139.
SS

Simon Sinek

b. 1973
Popularised purpose-first communication through the golden circle.
Golden Circle
Sinek, S. (2009) Start with why: how great leaders inspire everyone to take action. London: Portfolio Penguin.
SET

Stephen E. Toulmin

1922–2009
Philosopher whose argument model exposes the missing warrant behind most weak business claims.
Toulmin Model
Toulmin, S.E. (2003) The uses of argument. Updated edn. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.
ARJ

Al Ries & Jack Trout

2001
Argued that positioning happens in the mind of the customer, not in the product specification.
Positioning Statement
Ries, A. and Trout, J. (2001) Positioning: the battle for your mind. 20th anniversary edn. New York: McGraw-Hill.
BWT

Bruce W. Tuckman

1938–2016
Described the forming–storming–norming–performing sequence every new team passes through.
Group Development
Tuckman, B.W. (1965) 'Developmental sequence in small groups', Psychological Bulletin, 63(6), pp. 384–399.
KNL

Katherine N. Lemon & Peter C. Verhoef

2016
Consolidated customer experience research into a journey model spanning pre-purchase to post-purchase.
Customer Journey Mapping
Lemon, K.N. and Verhoef, P.C. (2016) 'Understanding customer experience throughout the customer journey', Journal of Marketing, 80(6), pp. 69–96.
HW

Heinz Weihrich

b. 1936
Converted the static SWOT into the TOWS matrix, the step where analysis becomes strategy.
TOWS Matrix
Weihrich, H. (1982) 'The TOWS matrix — a tool for situational analysis', Long Range Planning, 15(2), pp. 54–66.
SG

Sunil Gupta et al.

2006
Established the modelling foundations of customer lifetime value as a discounted asset rather than a marketing slogan.
CLV / CAC
Gupta, S., Hanssens, D., Hardie, B., Kahn, W., Kumar, V., Lin, N., Ravishanker, N. and Sriram, S. (2006) 'Modeling customer lifetime value', Journal of Service Research, 9(2), pp. 139–155.
GJ

Gerry Johnson et al.

2020
Authors of the strategy text that codified PESTEL in its modern six-factor teaching form.
PESTEL Analysis
Johnson, G., Whittington, R., Regnér, P., Scholes, K. and Angwin, D. (2020) Exploring strategy: text and cases. 12th edn. Harlow: Pearson.
EL

Edmund Learned et al.

1969
The Harvard group who assembled the SWOT logic into business policy teaching.
SWOT Analysis
Learned, E.P., Christensen, C.R., Andrews, K.R. and Guth, W.D. (1969) Business policy: text and cases. Homewood, IL: Richard D. Irwin.
NS

Nigel Slack et al.

2019
Standard operations management text covering capacity, process design and quality planning.
Capacity Planning · RACI
Slack, N., Brandon-Jones, A. and Burgess, N. (2019) Operations management. 9th edn. Harlow: Pearson.
